# 🖥️ Crispy Chikis - Restaurant Management App  

**Crispy Chikis - Restaurant Management App** is the desktop platform that allows the restaurant to efficiently manage its operations. It complements the **mobile app** by giving administrators full control over products, orders, and customer interactions.  

## 🌍 Features  
- 🛒 Add, edit, or remove products from the menu.  
- 📝 Attach comments or notes to specific items or orders.  
- 📥 Receive and manage customer orders in real time.  
- 📊 View past orders and daily reports.
